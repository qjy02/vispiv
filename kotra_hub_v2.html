📢 Replacement Holiday – HQ only
31 August & 1 September 2026

Further to memo dated 16 March 2026 regarding the additional public holiday in conjunction with Hari Raya Aidilfitri, please be informed that the company has designated 1 September 2026 (Tuesday) as the replacement holiday.

The company will be closed on 31 August 2026 (Monday) & 1 September 2026 (Tuesday). Operation will resume on 2 September 2026 (Wednesday).

⚠️ Employees who take medical leave (MC) immediately before or after this replacement holiday may be subject to a six (6) months suspension of outpatient medical card benefits, in accordance with the company's policies.

Should you have any queries, kindly refer to the HR Department.

Cuti Gantian – HQ sahaja

Merujuk kepada memo bertarikh 16 Mac 2026 mengenai cuti umum tambahan sempena Hari Raya Aidilfitri, dimaklumkan bahawa syarikat telah menetapkan 1 September 2026 (Selasa) sebagai cuti gantian.
